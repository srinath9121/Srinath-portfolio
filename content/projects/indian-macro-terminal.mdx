> **Development Status:** Currently In Progress / Under Development. This system is an active engineering initiative and is not presented as a production deployment.

## Project Scope

Indian Macro Terminal is an institutional-style macroeconomic and market intelligence workstation focused on India. The project connects macroeconomic developments, global risk factors, commodity dynamics, market anomalies, transmission pathways, and potential market exposures into a unified analytical surface.

## Core Modules

The workstation is organized around seven integrated modules:

1. **Market Intelligence:** Real-time and structural tracking of market movements, liquidity indicators, and cross-asset dynamics.
2. **Macro & RBI:** Systematic tracking of central bank policy statements, monetary indicators, inflation metrics, and liquidity operations.
3. **Global Risk:** Monitoring international transmission channels, foreign portfolio flows, exchange rate pressures, and systemic risk indices.
4. **Commodities:** Analysis of crude oil, industrial metals, and agricultural commodity cycles impacting Indian input costs and fiscal parameters.
5. **Transmission Analysis:** Tracing policy and macroeconomic shocks through domestic interest rate structures, credit markets, and equity sectors.
6. **Anomaly Detection:** Algorithmic detection of price dislocations, atypical market behaviors, and divergence between macro indicators and asset pricing.
7. **AI Investigator:** Context-aware analytical assistant to synthesize research notes, inspect transmission graphs, and query structured economic series.

## Technology Stack

The terminal utilizes a lightweight, restrained architecture:

- **FastAPI:** High-performance asynchronous API layer serving analytical queries, time-series transformations, and econometric calculations.
- **React:** Component-based user interface optimized for dense financial and economic information displays.
- **SQLite:** Embedded, zero-configuration relational database engineered for local analytical caching, time-series partitions, and indicator schemas.
